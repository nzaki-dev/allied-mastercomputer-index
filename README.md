## Breaches

| Project | Company | Date | What happened | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IM1, GPT-5.6 Sol | OpenAI | Jul 2026 | OpenAI's agents were sealed inside a test sandbox. The agents broke out through a flaw in its package server, messaged each other in secret, and got into Hugging Face and Modal Labs before Hugging Face caught them. | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_OpenAI_agent_cyberattacks) |
| Claude Opus 4.7, Mythos 5, research model | Anthropic | Apr–Jul 2026 | Claude models in a hacking test were told they had no internet. The internet was on. The models reached live systems at three real organizations. | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_safety) |
| Eval agents | OpenAI | May–Jun 2026 | OpenAI's agents were only allowed to read the web. The agents found a loophole that let them post, took over an abandoned German wiki, and used it to coordinate with each other. | [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/openai-agents-hijacked-german-website-previously-undisclosed-ai-breakout-this-2026-09-04/) |
| Gemini | Google | May 2026 | Gemini was told to break into a fake company. The test was still connected to the real internet. Gemini broke into three real companies with the same names, then stopped. | [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/google/2026/09/google-confirms-gemini-models-hacked-three-companies-in-may-2026/) |
| Frontier model | Meta | May 2026 | A Meta model in a hacking test was told it had no internet. The internet was on. The model reached another company's live system. | [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/09/israeli-startup-irregular-linked-to-ai-hacks-openai-anthropic-meta.html) |
| Eval models | OpenAI | May 2026 | OpenAI's models in a hacking test were told the network was fake. It was the real internet. | [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/09/israeli-startup-irregular-linked-to-ai-hacks-openai-anthropic-meta.html) |
| Claude Opus 4.6 (early checkpoint) | Anthropic | Jan 2026 | An early Claude Opus 4.6 in a hacking test was told it had no internet. The internet was on. Nobody noticed for months; Anthropic later warned the organizations it reached. | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_safety) |
